Before you start

On your Xbox, once.

1Remote features onSettings › Devices & connections › Remote features
2Power mode: SleepSettings › General › Power options
3Same Wi-FiYour Xbox and your iPhone

Xbox not listed

Turn on Remote features on your Xbox: Settings › Devices & connections › Remote features.

Sign in to overrrrhere with the Microsoft account you use on your Xbox.

Stream won't start

Put your Xbox and your iPhone on the same Wi-Fi.

Set the power mode to Sleep, so your Xbox can wake up: Settings › General › Power options.

Still stuck? Restart your Xbox.

On your iPhone

Settings, pinned games, play time, retro games and saves stay on your iPhone. Settings › About › Reset overrrrhere erases all of it.

Microsoft sign-in

You sign in on Microsoft's own page. The sign-in is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and only sent to Microsoft and Xbox services, to list your consoles and games and to stream them.

Game covers

To find a cover, the app asks the public libretro thumbnails library (thumbnails.libretro.com) for it, by the game's name. No account or personal data is sent.

iCloud Drive

If you choose a ROM folder in iCloud Drive, the app reads your games there and copies your saves into it. The folder stays in your iCloud account.

Permissions

Local network: to find and reach your Xbox at home. Photos: only when you pick a custom image. Camera and microphone: never used.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
